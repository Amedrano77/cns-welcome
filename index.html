Workday onboarding

Your onboarding tasks, one card at a time.

All of these live in Workday at login.tstc.edu. Open only the card you're working on right now.

  • Log in at login.tstc.edu with your TSTC username and password.
  • Find the "Awaiting Your Action" card on your home page and click "Go to My Tasks."
  • Or click the "My Tasks" inbox icon near your profile picture (upper-left).
  • Open a task → click "E-sign by Adobe Sign" → Continue / Start to read through it.
  • At the signature block: type your name → Apply → Click to Sign → Submit.
💡
Each task has a short description at the top. Read it before signing — you can always review the document first.
  • Open "Consent: Consent to Receive Form 1098-T Electronically."
  • Read the info — 1098-T reports your qualified tuition and may help with education tax credits.
  • Check the box: "I consent to receive my IRS Form 1098-T electronically."
  • Click Submit. You'll see a confirmation.
📅
Once consented, your form is available in Workday each year by January 31.
  • Open the form and click E-sign by Adobe Sign → Continue.
  • Click Start, answer each question (citizenship, Texas residency, etc.), click Next.
  • Type your name and student ID in the boxes provided.
  • Click "Click to sign," type your full name, click Apply, and enter the date (MM/DD/YYYY).
  • Click Submit, then Done.
⚠️
You may need to complete this each academic year. Incomplete forms can delay your aid — questions to Financial Aid: 325-734-3608.
  • Open "Student Residency Event" and click Start Questionnaire.
  • Answer each question honestly — some students get extra sections based on their answers.
  • Work through all sections until you reach the signature page.
  • Type your full name to sign, then Submit.
⚠️
Answers must be accurate and may need documentation. Inaccurate answers can cause reclassification and fees. The Registrar's Office reviews your submission and notifies you by TSTC email.
  • Open "Academic Fresh Start Questionnaire" from your inbox and read the information.
  • Click Next, then answer Yes or No to whether you want to establish an academic fresh start.
  • Click Submit, then Done.
ℹ️
Brand-new to TSTC? You won't see this task — skip it. It's only for students returning after an absence. Answering "No" never hurts your enrollment.
  • Open "TSIA2 Pre-Assessment" and click Start.
  • Pick a subject: Reading, Writing, or Math.
  • Answer the questions — it's not timed and there's no penalty for guessing.
  • Review your results — they show whether you're ready for the TSIA2 or could use some prep.
😌
This is not a pass/fail test and doesn't affect your admission. It just points you to the right starting line.
  • Open "Consent: TSI Assessment Virtual" and click Next.
  • Read the virtual testing requirements (quiet space, photo ID, working webcam & mic).
  • Check the box to agree to the terms, then Submit.
  • You can now schedule your virtual TSI Assessment — check your email for details.
🎧
You must consent each time you test virtually. Testing Center: 325-734-3641 · 650 Hwy 80 E, Room 112.

Supplies & books.

Just the essentials to get going. One required item, a couple of optional kits, and your first-semester books are already covered.

💻 Your laptop / desktop

  • 32 GB RAM recommended
  • CPU: 1 GHz+ 64-bit, 2+ cores (Intel 8th Gen+ / AMD Ryzen 2000+)
  • 128 GB USB drive or higher Required
  • USB-C Ethernet adapter (if your laptop has no Ethernet port)
🚫 No Chromebooks, iPads, Surface Pros, or MacBooks.

🧰 Handy tool kits

  • PC Repair Kit — Amazon ≈ $9 Optional
  • Network Tool Kit — Amazon ≈ $27 Optional
🛠️
Neither kit is required — just nice to have if you want to tinker at home.

Three paths. Endless possibilities.

Pick your pathway.

Every student starts the same way — Semester 1 is identical across all three paths. Where you go from there depends on how far you want to take it. You can always keep going.

🖥️

Certificate 1

IT Support Tier I
8 Months18 Credits

Your fastest entry into IT. Two semesters and you're job-ready with foundational networking and PC skills.

Career outcomes
  • IT Support Specialist
  • Help Desk Technician
  • Desktop Support
  • Junior Network Technician
🔀

Certificate 2

IT Support Tier II
12 Months30 Credits

Builds on Certificate 1 with CCNA networking, Linux, virtualization, and security — stepping into higher-tier roles.

Career outcomes
  • Network Technician
  • Systems Administrator
  • Security Technician
  • Virtualization Specialist
🎓

AAS Degree

Associate of Applied Science
20 Months60 Credits

The full degree. Five semesters covering enterprise networking, cloud, security, server admin, and a capstone or internship.

Career outcomes

  • Systems Administrator
  • Cybersecurity Analyst
  • Cloud Administrator
  • IT Project Coordinator
